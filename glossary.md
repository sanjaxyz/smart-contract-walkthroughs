# Glossary

This glossary defines common terms used in the contract walkthroughs.

## Balance
The amount of tokens owned by a specific address.

## Allowance
A value that specifies how many tokens one address is permitted to transfer on behalf of another address.

## Total Supply
The total number of tokens that exist at a given time.

## Event
A record emitted by a smart contract that allows external systems to observe contract activity.

## Address
An identifier representing an account or smart contract on Ethereum.
